Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

ProfitFlowOS offers subscription-based access to analytics and business intelligence tools for digital creators and operators.

Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Access to the platform remains active until the end of the current billing cycle.

Annual Subscriptions

Annual plans are discounted and generally non-refundable after purchase. However, refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis if submitted within 7 days of the original purchase.

Cancellation

Users may cancel their subscription through their account settings or through the Stripe billing portal. After cancellation, the subscription will remain active until the end of the billing period.

Exceptions

Refunds may be granted in cases of billing errors or technical issues that prevent access to the service.

Contact

For refund requests or billing questions contact: support@profitflowos.com
